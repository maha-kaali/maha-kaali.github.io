# **About Me**

Hey I'm <b>Jayasree M</b>:wave:,<br>
<p align="justify">
Part builder, part thinker, full-time curious human.
<br>
<br>

I’m drawn to the kind of messy problems where AI has to make decisions under pressure or outsmart chaos. Right now, that means I'm deep into AI security and active defense, specifically engineering ways to outsmart deepfakes (proactive defence) and building out intelligent, agentic systems that can handle real-world adversarial chaos.
<br>
For me, the end goal isn't just a paper or a proof of concept, it's about building deep-tech that actually scales and functions in the wild. I love the transition from a complex math problem to a deployable, real-world system, and eventually, a startup.
<br>
I don’t claim to have all the answers, just a growing list of questions and a weirdly high tolerance for chasing them down. Whether it’s a bug, a roughly sketched architecture idea, or a broken edge case, I’m would be poking at it until it makes sense.</p>
